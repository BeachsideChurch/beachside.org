sermon_recap:
  - "We naturally pray for God's protection in life, often asking Him to keep bad things from happening to us or our families. Yet Psalm 23 presents an honest reality: following the Shepherd on paths of righteousness will sometimes lead us directly into dark, difficult valleys. When we try to orchestrate our lives to avoid every potential valley, we carry the exhausting weight of being our own best hope—a subtle form of self-reliance that leads directly to worry, anxiety, and deep spiritual burnout."
  - "True spiritual protection is not immunity from hard circumstances, but preservation from the spiritual monsters in the valley—such as bitterness, contempt, anger, and cynicism—that threaten to deform our souls. Jesus came to guard our inner life and offer superabundant life marked by the fruit of the Spirit. As we surrender our need to control outcomes and run into the strong tower of God's character, we discover that the Shepherd walks with us, using every season to form us into peaceful, resilient followers of Christ."
