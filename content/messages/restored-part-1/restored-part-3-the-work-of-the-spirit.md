  Psalm 23 tells us that the Good Shepherd restores our souls. Restoration is not merely about feeling better
  or becoming a slightly improved version of ourselves. It is about becoming fully human again. Over time, our
  thoughts, emotions, habits, bodies, and relationships can become disordered. We drift into ways of living
  that leave us anxious, distracted, exhausted, and disconnected from God. The invitation of repentance is not
  shame but redirection. It is turning around and allowing God to lead us toward life.
