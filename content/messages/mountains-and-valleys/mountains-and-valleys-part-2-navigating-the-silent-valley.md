description: >-
  We live in a culture obsessed with curated perfection—pristine schedules, flawless kids, optimized mental
  health, and polished public images. Yet, when we read the story of Jesus, we discover that He never promises
  us a friction-free, comfortable life. In fact, He guarantees that trouble will find us. But right alongside
  that warning, He offers a far deeper guarantee: His unshakeable presence. Presence will always be infinitely
  better than preference. Having someone who loves you stand beside you in the middle of a brutal storm is far
  sweeter than standing alone in pleasant weather. Psalm 23 captures this reality by reminding us that even
  when we walk through the valley of the shadow of death, we fear no evil—not because the scary shadows vanish
  overnight, but because the Shepherd walks directly beside us.
