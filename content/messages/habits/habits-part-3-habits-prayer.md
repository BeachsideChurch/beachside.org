description: >-
  Every one of us is being formed by the paths we walk every day. Some of those paths are obvious—the habits
  we’ve intentionally built into our lives. Others are so familiar that we no longer notice them. They are the
  patterns of thought we continually return to, the ways we respond to disappointment, the routines that
  quietly shape our priorities, and the practices that slowly become our character. Psalm 23 reminds us that
  our Good Shepherd does not simply point us toward the right path and hope we find our way. He lovingly
  guides us there. The encouraging truth is that no matter what paths have shaped us in the past, we are not
  destined to stay on them forever. By God’s grace, we can begin walking new paths that lead us toward the
  life He desires for us.
