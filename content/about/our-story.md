    body: |
      In 2007, a small group of friends began gathering with a shared desire to create a church that
      unchurched people would love to attend. They connected with the leadership of North Point Ministries in
      Atlanta and Beachside launched as a Strategic Partner church.

      By the summer of 2008, small groups began to form around Fort Walton Beach. Beachside held its first
      public service in August 2009.

      Beachside began as a portable church at the Boys & Girls Club of the Emerald Coast, then moved to
      Choctawhatchee High School as it grew. In 2013, Beachside purchased the former Northwest Florida Daily
      News building. Renovations made possible by sacrificial giving and service led to the first phase
      opening in summer 2014.

      The mission of Beachside Church is to inspire people to follow Jesus with all of their heart. **This is
      just the beginning.**
