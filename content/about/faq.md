title: FAQ
description: Frequently asked questions about Beachside Church.
layout: landing
sections:
  - kind: columns
    columns:
      - title: What is your relationship with North Point?
        body: >-
          Beachside Church is a separate organization and a Strategic Partner of North Point Ministries. We
          share the same mission, strategy, values, and beliefs.
      - title: What happens on Sundays?
        body: >-
          A typical service includes live worship and practical Bible teaching. Messages may be delivered live
          at Beachside or shared from North Point Community Church.
      - title: What is your mission?
        body: >-
          Our mission is to inspire people to follow Jesus with all of their heart by creating environments
          that help people pursue intimacy with God, community with insiders, and influence with outsiders.
      - title: Are you part of a denomination?
        body: >-
          Beachside is non-denominational. We aim to create an attractive, understandable environment for
          people who do not regularly attend church.
      - title: How is Beachside governed?
        body: >-
          We are guarded by Elders, guided by staff, and gifted through attendees. Stewardship, Leadership,
          and Elder teams provide financial, operational, and spiritual oversight.
      - title: Why are groups so important?
        body: >-
          We believe spiritual growth happens best in authentic community. Home-based groups provide
          consistent relationships, encouragement, and accountability.
